 # Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 21, 2025**

This Privacy Policy explains how this project ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the project, its website, applications, or related services (collectively, the "Services").

We may collect:

- **Information you provide**, such as your name, email address, account details, messages, and other information you submit through the Services.
- **Usage and technical information**, such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages or features viewed, referring URLs, dates and times of access, and diagnostic data.
- **Cookies and similar technologies.** We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the Services, remember preferences, understand usage, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect functionality.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the feature you are using.

We use information to:

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use personal information for purposes incompatible with this policy without notice or, where required, your consent.

We may share information with service providers that help us host, analyze, secure, or operate the Services; with professional advisers; in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets; or when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or the Services. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting, security, and dispute-resolution requirements. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of your personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. You may also unsubscribe from non-essential marketing communications at any time. To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the details below. We may verify your request before responding.
